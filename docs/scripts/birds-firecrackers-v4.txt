Why do birds burst into the sky when a firecracker goes off?
A bird's nervous system treats a sudden explosion as a possible life-threatening event, not entertainment.
The bang fires its threat-detection system, and its body flips into the same emergency fight-or-flight mode it uses for predators.
A human may know the bang comes from fireworks. A bird cannot make that call - to it, the blast might be a predator or a collision about to happen.
And it rarely stops at one bird: when one panics and takes off, the rest of the flock can follow, a chain reaction researchers call collective escape.
Radar over the Netherlands has caught hundreds of thousands of birds in the air at once on New Year's Eve, many abandoning their night roosts for the dark sky.
