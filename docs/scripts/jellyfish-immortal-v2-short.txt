What if I told you there's a creature nicknamed the immortal jellyfish, almost like a vampire? Spoiler: it's not a bat.
Meet Turritopsis dohrnii. Most jellyfish grow one way: larva, polyp, adult. Under stress, like injury or starvation, this one reverses its life cycle: its bell shrinks, tentacles disappear, and the body reorganises back into a juvenile polyp. The secret is transdifferentiation - specialised cells change identity to rebuild the organism.
The catch? Immortal doesn't mean unkillable. Predators eat it; disease kills it too. Reversals have been observed repeatedly, but no individual was ever proven to live forever.
So if you could reverse your biological age just once, would you go back to childhood? And don't say it's just to avoid paying bills!
