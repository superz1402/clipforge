When a firecracker suddenly explodes, birds sitting peacefully on trees take off in every direction within seconds. But why such panic?
The blast hits their danger-detection system and triggers fight-or-flight: stress hormones surge, muscles prepare, the body readies to escape. And birds don't know fireworks are entertainment - a sudden explosion can signal danger nearby, like a predator attack. One bird taking flight can trigger hundreds to follow: collective escape behaviour. At night, that panic means disorientation, collisions, and wasted energy.
So if birds could understand why we burst firecrackers, would they forgive us, or officially put humans on their list of natural disasters?
