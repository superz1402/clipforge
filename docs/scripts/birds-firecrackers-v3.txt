Why do birds burst into the sky when a firecracker goes off?
On New Year's Eve in the Netherlands, weather radar watched hundreds of thousands of birds take off within minutes of the fireworks starting.
Loud, sudden blasts startle birds into flight - researchers measured it directly by firing blank cannon charges near roosting blackbirds.
Fireworks strike at the worst time: most birds are roosting, so a quiet night erupts into panicked flight.
In that Dutch study, birds climbed far higher than usual, and the reaction was still strong ten kilometers from the fireworks.
Big birds in open country reacted the most - ducks, geese and gulls rising off wetlands in huge numbers.
So it is not sharper hearing: a sudden blast at night panics resting birds off their roosts, and they fly.
