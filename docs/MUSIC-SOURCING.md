# Music sourcing route (research, Nov 16 2026)

Requirement (user): background music stays in the videos, but only music we have
verified permission to use, with the license record saved. No spending.

## Chosen primary source: incompetech.com (Kevin MacLeod), CC BY 4.0

Why this source:
- Direct from the composer, not an aggregator. Provenance is one hop.
- License verified on the site 2026-11-16: "Creative Commons - Free. No charge.
  Requires that you credit the music." Credit block generated by the site:
  "<Title>" / Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com) /
  Licensed under Creative Commons: By Attribution 4.0 /
  http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- CC BY 4.0 explicitly allows commercial use and modification (trimming/looping),
  which covers YouTube Shorts including a monetized channel.
- Cost: $0. The paid "Standard License" ($30/track) only removes the attribution
  requirement; we can comply with attribution, so no spend is needed.
- Full catalog is machine-readable (pieces.json, 1443 tracks) with feel/BPM/ISRC
  metadata, so future picks are fast and documented the same way.

Attribution placement: video description on every upload that uses a track.

## Residual risk, stated honestly

A CC BY license does not make Content ID claims impossible (third parties have
wrongly claimed CC tracks before). Difference from the HAAWK incident: we now hold
a license record per track, so a claim would be met with evidence - and our policy
stays: no disputes without verified rights, and we can always fall back to
narration-only.

## Sources checked and rejected for now

- FreePD.com (Kevin MacLeod's CC0 public-domain site): CLOSED, taken offline.
  Do not use copies of FreePD files from mirrors - provenance chain broken.
- MPT bundled resource/songs/: untagged, no license info. Banned (see MUSIC.md).
- Mixkit: currently throttled for us; also its license is site-specific, revisit later.
- Pixabay/aggregator "no copyright music": license terms are site-wide, not
  composer-direct; third-party Content ID claims on such tracks are common.
  Only reconsider with per-track evidence.
- YouTube Audio Library: legitimate per-track licenses, but downloading needs the
  signed-in YouTube Studio account. Viable later; incompetech is simpler and
  account-free, so it is the primary route.
